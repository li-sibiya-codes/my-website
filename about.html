About

More than just code.

I'm a third-year computer science and applied mathematics student at the University of KwaZulu-Natal. I like working between the two because maths and software have a way of exposing different sides of the same problem.

So far, my studies and interests have taken me through application development, databases, concurrent systems, machine learning, and quantum computing. More often than not, something comes up that wasn't on the syllabus. I usually end up reading about it, then building something to see if the idea holds up. Sometimes it does. Sometimes the part that breaks is the useful part.

I have a few ideas in mind for where I might specialise, and I'm in no rush to pick one yet. For now, I'm trying things out, learning from the people around me, and following whatever keeps my attention. Wherever I end up, I'd like it to be somewhere with hard problems and people who are happy to think out loud.
